Part III – Problem Solving

Frederick is 6 feet tall and casts an 8-foot shadow. A tree casts a 24-foot shadow. How long would the tree's shadow be if the tree were 6 feet taller?

Tree: 6/8 = h/24 → h = 18 ft. Taller tree 24 ft: 6/8 = 24/s → s = 32 ft.
