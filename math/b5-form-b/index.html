6.
Maya walked to the library, studied there, and then walked back home. The graph below shows her distance from home (feet) over time (minutes). For each part, explain how you know your answer is correct.
  1. How far away is the library from Maya’s house?
    3,000 feet. The flat part of the graph is at 3,000 feet.
  2. How long did Maya study at the library?
    45 minutes. The graph is flat from 10 to 55 minutes: 55 − 10 = 45.
  3. Find Maya’s rate of speed in feet per minute for the walk to the library.
    300 feet per minute. 3,000 ft ÷ 10 min = 300.
  4. Find Maya’s rate of speed in feet per minute for the walk home.
    200 feet per minute. She walks 3,000 ft from minute 55 to minute 70: 3,000 ÷ 15 = 200.

13.
Ana says Graph B models direct variation and has a rate of 32. Liam says Graph A models direct variation because it passes through the origin. Which person is correct? Explain your reasoning.
Ana is correct. Graph B is a straight line through (0, 0), and y ÷ x = 3 ÷ 2 = 6 ÷ 4 = 3/2. Graph A passes through the origin but is a curve, not a straight line (1 ÷ 2 = ½ but 4 ÷ 4 = 1), so it is not direct variation.

How many bracelets will Jada need to sell to earn $81.00?

18 bracelets. Rate = 13.50 ÷ 3 = $4.50 per bracelet (27 ÷ 6 = 4.50 too), so y = 4.5x. 81 = 4.5x → x = 81 ÷ 4.5 = 18. Check: 18 × 4.50 = 81.00.
