Part III – Problem Solving

There are 10 girls and 15 boys on the swim team. They swim laps in a swim-a-thon to raise money for new team suits. Each girl raised $6.00 per lap and swam at a rate of 12 laps per hour. Each boy raised $4.00 per lap and swam at a rate of 2 laps every 15 minutes. What is the ratio of the amount of money the girls raised in one hour to the amount of money the boys raised in one hour?

Girls: 10 girls × 12 laps × $6 = $720 per hour. Boys: 2 laps per 15 min = 8 laps per hour (60 ÷ 15 = 4, 2 × 4 = 8); 15 boys × 8 laps × $4 = $480 per hour. Ratio 720 : 480 = 3 : 2 (divide both by 240).
