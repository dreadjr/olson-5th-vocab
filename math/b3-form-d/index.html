Part III – Problem Solving

A store pays $80 for a lamp. It marks the price up 60% to set its selling price. Later, the lamp goes on sale for 25% off the selling price. Maya says the sale price is 35% more than the $80 the store paid, because 60% − 25% = 35%. Is Maya correct? What is the overall percent of change from the $80 the store paid to the sale price? Use mathematics to justify your answer.

Selling price: 0.6 × 80 = $48 mark-up, so $80 + $48 = $128. Sale: 0.25 × 128 = $32 off, so $128 − $32 = $96. Change: $96 − $80 = $16 increase; 16 ÷ 80 = 0.20 = 20% increase. Maya is not correct (35% would be $108). The 25% is taken off $128, not off $80. Check: 1.6 × 0.75 = 1.2, so the sale price is 120% of $80.
