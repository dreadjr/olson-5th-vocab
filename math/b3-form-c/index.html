Part III – Problem Solving

A couch was originally $400. It was on sale for 40% off the original price. When the Garcia family bought the couch, they found out it was an additional 25% off the sale price. What was the overall percent of discount from the original price of the couch to the price the Garcias paid?

Sale price: 0.6 × 400 = $240. Then 25% off: 0.75 × 240 = $180. Savings: $400 − $180 = $220. Overall discount: 220 ÷ 400 = 0.55 = 55% (not 65%). Check: 0.6 × 0.75 = 0.45, so they paid 45% of the original.
