  • A. 45, 3 : 4, 7 to 10
  • B. 3 : 4, 7 to 10, 45

C (as decimals: 7 to 10 = 0.7, 3 : 4 = 0.75, 45 = 0.8)

A hockey team has a win to loss ratio of 3 : 4. Which could be the team’s record?
  • A. 6 wins and 14 games played
  • B. 8 wins and 6 losses
  • C. 9 wins and 12 games played
  • D. 12 wins and 15 losses
A (14 − 6 = 8 losses; 6 : 8 = 3 : 4. B is 4 : 3, C is 9 : 3 = 3 : 1, D is 4 : 5)

  • A. 6 bars for $4.80

D (price per bar: A $0.80, B $0.75, C $0.75, D $8.40 ÷ 12 = $0.70, the lowest)

C (1 pound = 16 ounces, not 12, so the top and bottom are not the same amount)

Part III – Problem Solving

The garden club has 6 parents and 15 students. They pull weeds at a park to raise money for new seeds, and a sponsor pays for each bag of weeds they fill. Each parent earned $4.00 per bag and filled one bag every 12 minutes. Each student earned $3.00 per bag and filled 3 bags every 45 minutes. What is the ratio of the amount of money the parents raised in one hour to the amount of money the students raised in one hour?

Parents: one bag every 12 min = 5 bags per hour (60 ÷ 12 = 5); 6 parents × 5 bags × $4 = $120 per hour. Students: 3 bags per 45 min = 4 bags per hour (45 min = 34 hr, 3 ÷ 34 = 4); 15 students × 4 bags × $3 = $180 per hour. Ratio 120 : 180 = 2 : 3 (divide both by 60).
