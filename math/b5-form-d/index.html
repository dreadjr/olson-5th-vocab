Which story best matches the speed graph below?
  • A. Ella walked to the park, sat on a bench, and then walked home.
  • B. A runner ran at the same speed the whole time.
  • C. A skateboarder slowed down, stopped, and then sped up again.
  • D. A bus sped up, drove at a steady speed, then slowed down and stopped at a bus stop.
D. On a speed graph, rising means speeding up, flat means a steady speed, and falling to 0 means slowing to a stop. (A describes a distance graph: the flat part there would mean stopped.)
3.
If the graph below models direct variation, what is its rate?
  • A. 27
  • B. 3.5
  • C. 7
  • D. The graph does not model direct variation.
B. 3.5. The line is straight and passes through (0, 0), so it is direct variation. Rate = y ÷ x = 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5. (2/7 is x ÷ y, the wrong way round.)

6.
Zoe rode her bike to a friend’s house. The graph below shows her speed (miles per hour) over time (minutes). For each part, explain how you know your answer is correct.
  1. What was Zoe’s fastest speed?
    12 miles per hour. The highest part of the graph is at 12.
  2. For how many minutes did Zoe ride at her fastest speed?
    8 minutes. The graph is flat at 12 from minute 2 to minute 10: 10 − 2 = 8.
  3. What was happening to Zoe’s speed from minute 10 to minute 12?
    She slowed down from 12 mph to 6 mph (a drop of 12 − 6 = 6 mph). The graph goes down.
  4. What was Zoe doing from minute 12 to minute 18? Was she stopped?
    She rode at a steady (constant) speed of 6 mph. She was not stopped: a flat part on a speed graph means constant speed. She stopped at minute 20, where the speed reaches 0.

13.
Omar says Graph A models direct variation and has a rate of 4, because 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Priya says Graph B models direct variation because it is a straight line and has a rate of 12. Is either student correct? Explain each student’s mistake.
Neither student is fully correct. Omar is right that Graph A is direct variation (a straight line through (0, 0)), but he divided x ÷ y. The rate is y ÷ x = 2 ÷ 8 = 1 ÷ 4 = ¼. Priya is wrong: Graph B is a straight line, but it starts at (0, 3), so it does not pass through the origin and is not direct variation.
