1.
Which ratios form a proportion with 1220? Circle all that apply.
  • A. 1520
  • B. 915
  • C. 2012
  • D. 35
  • E. 2135
  • F. 1624
B, D, E (cross products: 12 · 15 = 20 · 9 = 180; 12 · 5 = 20 · 3 = 60; 12 · 35 = 20 · 21 = 420. A: 240 ≠ 300, C: 144 ≠ 400, F: 288 ≠ 320)
2.
A 6-pack of yogurt costs $4.50. A 10-pack of the same yogurt costs $6.90. How much less per cup does the 10-pack cost?
  • A. $0.06
  • B. $0.69
  • C. $0.75
  • D. $2.40
A ($4.50 ÷ 6 = $0.75 per cup; $6.90 ÷ 10 = $0.69 per cup; $0.75 − $0.69 = $0.06)
3.
Ana walked 3 miles in 1 hour 15 minutes. At this pace, how long would it take her to walk 7 miles?
  • A. 2 hours 20 minutes
  • B. 2 hours 41 minutes
  • C. 3 hours 5 minutes
  • D. 2 hours 55 minutes
D (1 h 15 min = 75 min; 3/75 = 7/x → 3x = 525 → x = 175 min = 2 h 55 min)

5.
Maya used a mirror to find the height of a flagpole. Her eye height is 5 feet and she stands 4 feet from the mirror. The mirror is 28 feet from the base of the flagpole. How tall is the flagpole?

  • B. 29 feet
  • C. 35 feet
  • D. 140 feet
C (h/5 = 28/4 → 4h = 140 → h = 35 ft)

7.
Two similar hexagons have perimeters of 18 cm and 30 cm. For numbers 7a – 7d, determine whether each statement about the hexagons is true or false.
7a. Their scale factor is 3 : 5.TRUEFALSE 7b. The ratio of their areas is 9 : 25.TRUEFALSE 7c. Two corresponding sides could be 6 cm and 12 cm.TRUEFALSE 7d. If the smaller hexagon has an area of 18 cm², the larger has an area of 30 cm².TRUEFALSE

Part II – Constructed Response

1.
Solve the proportion. 2.4x = 615
x = 6 (6x = 2.4 · 15 = 36; check 2.4/6 = 0.4 = 6/15)
2.
Jonah says x = 9 is the solution to 410 = x25. Use cross products to decide whether he is correct. If he is not, find the correct value.
Not correct: 4 · 25 = 100 but 10 · 9 = 90, and 100 ≠ 90. Correct: 10x = 100 → x = 10.
3.
In the school band, the ratio of brass players to woodwind players is 4 : 7. There are 28 woodwind players. How many brass players are in the band? Show all work necessary to justify your answer.
16 brass players (4/7 = b/28 → 7b = 112 → b = 16)
4.
Store A sells 12 ounces of trail mix for $4.20. Store B sells 20 ounces of the same trail mix for $6.60. Which store has the better buy? How much would 30 ounces cost at the better price? Use mathematics to justify your answer.
Store B. A: $4.20 ÷ 12 = $0.35 per ounce. B: $6.60 ÷ 20 = $0.33 per ounce, which is less. 30 oz at Store B: 30 × $0.33 = $9.90.

10.
A 4-foot fence post casts a shadow 2.5 feet long. At the same time, a cell tower casts a shadow 40 feet long.
  1. Find the height of the cell tower. Use mathematics to justify your answer.
    64 feet (h/4 = 40/2.5 → 2.5h = 160 → h = 64)
  2. Liam measured the tower's shadow at noon and the post's shadow at 4 p.m. Explain why he cannot use these two shadows in a proportion.
    The sun's angle changes during the day, so shadows measured at different times do not form similar triangles. Both shadows must be measured at the same time.
11.
Use the similar rectangles below.
  1. Find their scale factor (small to large).
    2 : 5 (4 : 10)
  2. Find the ratio of their perimeters.
    2 : 5
  3. Find the ratio of their areas.
    4 : 25 (2² : 5²)
  4. The larger rectangle has an area of 150 m². What is the area of the smaller rectangle? Show all work necessary to justify your answer.
    24 m² (4/25 = x/150 → 25x = 600 → x = 24; check 4 × 6 = 24 and 10 × 15 = 150)
12.
Two similar triangles have perimeters of 9 feet and 15 feet. The larger triangle has an area of 50 ft². Omar used this information to find the area of the smaller triangle. Unfortunately, he made an error in his work. Explain the error and then find the correct area of the smaller triangle.
Scale factor is 9 : 15, which is 3 : 5
Ratio of their areas is 3² : 5², which is 9 : 25
Let x = the area of the smaller triangle
925 = 50x
x ≈ 138.9
The area of the smaller triangle is about 138.9 ft².
Omar put the larger area (50) with the smaller triangle's part of the ratio (9). The areas must be in the same order as the ratio: 9/25 = x/50 → 25x = 450 → x = 18. The smaller area is 18 ft². (His answer is bigger than 50, which cannot be right for the smaller triangle.)
13.
On a map, two towns that are 84 miles apart are 3½ inches apart.
  1. Find the map's scale as 1 inch : ____ miles.
    1 inch : 24 miles (3.5/84 = 1/x → 3.5x = 84 → x = 24)
  2. Two lakes are 2¼ inches apart on the same map. How many miles apart are the lakes? Show all work necessary to justify your answer.
    54 miles (1/24 = 2.25/x → x = 54)

Part III – Problem Solving

Ms. Ortega's blueprint for a new game room has a scale of 1 inch : 4 feet. On the blueprint, the room is 6¼ inches long and 4½ inches wide. New flooring costs $3.20 per square foot. How much will it cost to cover the whole floor?

Dev tried this problem. He found the blueprint area, 6¼ × 4½ = 28⅛ square inches, and multiplied by 4 to get a floor area of 112½ square feet. Explain Dev's mistake, then find the correct cost. Finally, a pool table in the room will be 9 feet long. How long should it be drawn on the blueprint? Show all work.

Length: 1/4 = 6.25/L → L = 25 ft. Width: 1/4 = 4.5/W → W = 18 ft. Area = 25 × 18 = 450 ft². Cost = 450 × $3.20 = $1,440. Dev's mistake: the scale works on lengths, not areas. Each length is 4 times larger, so the area is 4² = 16 times larger (28⅛ × 16 = 450), not 4 times. Scale each length first, then multiply. Pool table: 1/4 = x/9 → 4x = 9 → x = 2¼ in.
