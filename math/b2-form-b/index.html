Scale factor is 12 : 48, which is 1 : 4
Ratio of their areas is 1 : 4
Let x = the area of the larger rectangle
14 = 5x
x = 20
The area of the larger rectangle is 20 ft².
Marco used the scale factor as the ratio of the areas. The area ratio is the scale factor squared: 1² : 4² = 1 : 16. 1/16 = 5/x → x = 80. The larger area is 80 ft².

Part III – Problem Solving

Jada stands next to a light pole in a parking lot. She is 5 feet tall and her shadow along the ground has a length of 3 feet. At the same time, the light pole casts a shadow along the ground that has a length of 12 feet. What would the length of the light pole's shadow along the ground be if the pole were 5 feet shorter?

Pole now: h/5 = 12/3 → 3h = 60 → h = 20 ft. Five feet shorter: 20 − 5 = 15 ft. New shadow: 15/5 = s/3 → 5s = 45 → s = 9 ft. The shadow would be 9 feet long.
