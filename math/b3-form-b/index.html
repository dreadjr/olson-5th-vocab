Part III – Problem Solving

A winter coat was originally $120. It was on sale for 20% off the original price. When Elena got to the register, she found out it was an additional 25% off the sale price. What was the overall percent of discount from the original price of the coat to the price Elena paid?

Sale price: 0.8 × 120 = $96. Then 25% off: 0.75 × 96 = $72. Savings: $120 − $72 = $48. Overall discount: 48 ÷ 120 = 0.40 = 40% (not 45%). Check: 0.8 × 0.75 = 0.6, so she paid 60% of the original.
