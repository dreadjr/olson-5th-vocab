Rosa. Rosa: 12 ÷ 15 = 0.8 lap per minute. Kim: 18 ÷ 24 = 0.75 lap per minute. 0.8 > 0.75. (Or: Rosa takes 1.25 min per lap, Kim takes about 1.33 min per lap.)

10.
Leo wanted to know the height of an apartment building on his street. One morning, he measured the building's shadow along the ground to be 36 feet long. At the same time, he measured his own shadow to be 4 feet long. Leo is 5 feet tall. Find the height of the building. Use mathematics to justify your answer.
45 feet (h/5 = 36/4 → 4h = 180 → h = 45; the building's shadow is 9 times Leo's, so it is 9 × 5 = 45 ft tall)

Scale factor is 14 : 35, which is 2 : 5

Ava multiplied each number by 2 instead of squaring it. The area ratio is 2² : 5² = 4 : 25. 4/25 = 12/x → 4x = 300 → x = 75. The larger area is 75 m².

Part III – Problem Solving

Kayla is measuring a young oak tree in a park. She is 5 feet tall and her shadow along the ground has a length of 2 feet. At the same time, the oak tree casts a shadow along the ground that has a length of 14 feet. In a few years the tree will be 10 feet taller. How long would its shadow along the ground be then, at the same time of day?

Tree now: h/5 = 14/2 → 2h = 70 → h = 35 ft. Ten feet taller: 35 + 10 = 45 ft. New shadow: 45/5 = s/2 → 5s = 90 → s = 18 ft. The shadow would be 18 feet long.
