Part III – Problem Solving

The science club has 8 seventh graders and 18 eighth graders. They fold paper cranes for a hospital fundraiser, and a sponsor donates money for each crane. Each seventh grader earned $3.00 per crane and folded at a rate of 15 cranes per hour. Each eighth grader earned $2.00 per crane and folded at a rate of 4 cranes every 20 minutes. What is the ratio of the amount of money the seventh graders raised in one hour to the amount of money the eighth graders raised in one hour?

Seventh graders: 8 × 15 cranes × $3 = $360 per hour. Eighth graders: 4 cranes per 20 min = 12 cranes per hour (60 ÷ 20 = 3, 4 × 3 = 12); 18 × 12 cranes × $2 = $432 per hour. Ratio 360 : 432 = 5 : 6 (divide both by 72).
