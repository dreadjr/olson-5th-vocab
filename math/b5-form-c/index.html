6.
Dev’s family drove to a lake, spent time there, and then drove back home. The graph below shows their distance from home (miles) over time (hours). For each part, explain how you know your answer is correct.
  1. How far away is the lake from Dev’s house?
    120 miles. The flat part of the graph is at 120 miles.
  2. How long did the family stay at the lake?
    4 hours. The graph is flat from hour 2 to hour 6: 6 − 2 = 4.
  3. Find the family’s rate of speed in miles per hour for the drive to the lake.
    60 miles per hour. 120 mi ÷ 2 h = 60.
  4. Find the family’s rate of speed in miles per hour for the drive home.
    40 miles per hour. They drive 120 mi from hour 6 to hour 9: 120 ÷ 3 = 40.

13.
Ken says Graph A models direct variation because it is a straight line and has a rate of 1. Rosa says Graph B models direct variation and has a rate of 13. Which person is correct? Explain your reasoning.
Rosa is correct. Graph B is a straight line through (0, 0), and y ÷ x = 1 ÷ 3 = 2 ÷ 6 = ⅓. Graph A is a straight line, but it starts at (0, 2), so it does not pass through the origin and is not direct variation.
